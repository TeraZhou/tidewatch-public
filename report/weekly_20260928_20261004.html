BTC 实现波动年化约 21.1%（中等），期末 Deribit IV 约 36.5%，IV-RV 点差约 +15.4 个百分点，标签期权偏贵；ETH RV ~17.7% 而 IV ~49.6%，溢价更宽。BTC RV ~21.1% ann (mid), end IV ~36.5% (Deribit), IV-RV spread ~+15.4 pp → IV rich; ETH RV ~17.7% vs IV ~49.6%, even wider premium.

读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
IV 是期权市场对未来波动的定价，RV 是已实现路径的统计；当 IV 显著高于 RV，保护与事件溢价被标贵，卖方收溢价、买方付贵，常见于震荡或刚经历挤压后的定价惯性。IV prices expected future move; RV measures the path already taken. IV >> RV means protection/event premium is rich—sellers collect, buyers overpay—typical after squeezes or inside chop.

本期组合：This week: 中等 RV + 偏贵 IV + 偏震荡路径，组合上更贴近波动收割与均值回归土壤，而非单边趋势加速。Mid RV + rich IV + chop path favors vol-harvest / mean-reversion terrain over accelerated trend.

价格小涨而 IV 仍显著高于 RV，说明上涨更多由挤压/短覆盖推动，期权层并未因「已实现变稳」而立刻便宜下来。Price edged up while IV stayed rich to RV—move looked squeeze/short-cover driven; options layer did not cheapen just because realized stayed mid.

VIX 在传统括号内变动约 0，权益波动定价平静，与加密 IV 偏贵形成对照，强化本周「加密偏独立」的波动叙事。VIX ~0 change in the TradFi bracket—equity vol pricing calm—contrasting crypto’s rich IV and reinforcing the idiosyncratic vol story.
