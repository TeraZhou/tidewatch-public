④ 波动环境Volatility Regime

IV 对 RV · BTC · ETHIV vs RV · BTC · ETH

BTC 实现波动年化约 65%，期末 IV 约 43%，IV−RV 约 -22 个百分点，标签高实现波动 + 期权偏便宜；ETH RV 更高、IV 亦高于 BTC，波动层级整体抬升。BTC realized vol ~65% ann., end IV ~43%, IV−RV ~-22 pp—high realized with options cheap vs RV; ETH RV higher still and IV above BTC, vol regime elevated across the board.

读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
实现波动量的是已经走完的路径长度，IV 是期权定价的前瞻拥挤度；本周路径很陡（高 RV）而 IV 仍折价，意味着保险相对已发生波动并不贵，但并不保证折价持续。Realized vol measures path length already walked; IV is the forward crowding priced in options. A steep path (high RV) with IV still at a discount means insurance is not rich versus what already printed—without promising the discount lasts.

本期组合：This week: 高 RV + IV 折价 + 强趋势上涨同时出现：趋势跟随着享受方向，波动收割类策略则面临「已波动够大、权利金却不厚」的错配。High RV + IV discount + strong uptrend together: trend-followers ride direction, while vol-harvest styles face a mismatch of large already-realized moves against still-thin premium.

价格大涨段与 IV 期末水平并未同步飙到「贵」，折价标签仍在；若后续 RV 回落而 IV 横着，折价可能收窄，反之若再出急杀，IV 才有机会快速补定价。The sharp price leg did not drag end IV into rich territory—the cheap label held. If RV later cools while IV holds, the discount may tighten; another air-pocket could let IV re-price faster.

VIX 括号内约 +6.2%，美股偏弱时传统波动计略抬，与加密高 RV 同向偏闹，但资产价格上仍是股弱币强，波动共鸣并未强制价格联动。VIX ~+6.2% in its bracket—traditional vol gauge ticked up as equities softened, rhyming with crypto’s high RV in ‘busyness’ without forcing price linkage (still equity down, crypto up).
