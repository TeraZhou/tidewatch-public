读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
IV高于RV表示期权市场对未来波动的报价仍贵于刚走过的实现路径；在价格几乎走平的一周，这种溢价更像“保险不便宜”，而不是趋势加速的燃料。IV above RV means options still price future moves richer than the path just realized; with price nearly flat, that premium reads more like “insurance isn’t cheap” than fuel for a fresh trend leg.

本期组合：This week: 中等RV + IV溢价 + 价格横盘，组合上更贴近波动收割/均值回归语境，而非单边趋势 fol lowing；若溢价持续而价格不破区间，卖方舒适区与买方追涨杀跌都会受挤压。Mid RV + rich IV + flat price sits closer to vol-harvest / mean-reversion framing than trend-following; persistent premium inside a range squeezes both premium sellers’ comfort and momentum chasers.

价格周线几乎零收益，IV却仍高于RV约6个点，说明波动报价并未因“无事发生”而立刻坍塌，市场仍在为尾部或事件预留费用。Near-zero weekly price return while IV stayed ~6 pts over RV shows vol pricing did not collapse just because “nothing happened”—the tape still charged for tails or event risk.

VIX窗口约-4.6%，美股波动温和回落；BTC的IV−RV溢价与加密偏独立标签并置，传统恐慌降温并未同步抹平加密期权溢价。VIX window ~−4.6% as equity vol eased; set beside BTC’s IV−RV premium and the idiosyncratic label, softer TradFi fear did not automatically erase crypto option richness.
