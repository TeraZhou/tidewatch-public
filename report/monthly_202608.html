四、波动环境4. Volatility regime

IV 对 RV · BTC · ETHIV vs RV · BTC · ETH

本月实现波动偏高：BTC年化实现波动约41.5%，ETH约66.9%。月末BTC期权隐含波动约37.6%，与实现波动大致匹配（隐含减实现约-3.9个百分点），没有明显偏贵或偏便宜到极端。Realized vol ran elevated this month: BTC realized about 41.5% ann., ETH about 66.9%. End-month BTC implied vol about 37.6%, roughly matched to realized (IV−RV about -3.9 pts)—neither clearly rich nor cheap in the extreme.

读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
实现波动（RV）看的是过去一段时间价格实际蹦得有多厉害，像「已经发生的颠簸」。隐含波动（IV）来自期权价格，像市场愿意为未来颠簸付多少保费。IV明显高于RV时，期权相对偏贵；明显低于时，相对偏便宜；接近则大致匹配。Realized vol (RV) measures how hard price actually bounced—past bumps. Implied vol (IV) comes from options prices—what the market charges for future bumps. IV well above RV means options look relatively expensive; well below means relatively cheap; near each other means roughly fair.

本月组合：This month: 合读：价格大涨、实现波动抬高，但月末IV并未大幅高于RV，说明期权保费没有在高波动之后明显溢价堆积；波动「发生了」，定价侧大致跟上而不是脱节。Together: a large price advance lifted realized vol, yet end-month IV did not sit far above RV—option premia did not pile into a rich premium after the move; vol happened, and pricing roughly kept pace rather than disconnecting.

大涨月里实现波动抬升很常见；本月末IV略低于RV一点，更像「波动已经实现、保费未明显加价」，而不是恐慌式抢保险。Elevated realized vol is common in a strong-up month; IV slightly under RV at month-end reads more like “vol already delivered, premia not heavily marked up” than a scramble for insurance.

传统侧VIX本月约-9.8%，股指波动预期在回落；加密实现波动却偏高。两边都偏「风险偏好回暖」，但加密价格弹性与已实现颠簸远大于股指，波动故事并不完全同一节奏。TradFi VIX fell about 9.8% this month as equity vol expectations eased; crypto realized vol stayed high. Both sides leaned risk-on, but crypto’s price elasticity and delivered bumps far exceeded equities—the vol stories were not identical in tempo.

七、三句收束7. Three takeaways

  1. 本月主线是加密大幅上攻（BTC约+25%、ETH约+32%），与美股温和risk-on同向，但弹性大得多；月中加速段贡献了主要涨幅。The month’s through-line was a large crypto advance (BTC ~+25%, ETH ~+32%), same direction as mild equity risk-on but with far higher beta; the mid-month surge delivered most of the gains.
  2. 现货ETF呈强流入（BTC净流入约+35.39亿美金），可与偏热的现货/永续成交并陈；杠杆上费率正、OI略降、月中有过清算峰值，更像上涨中的出清痕迹而非全面失控。Spot ETFs showed strong inflow (BTC net about +$3.54B), set beside lively spot/perp turnover; leverage had positive funding, slightly lower OI, and a mid-month liquidation peak—clearance traces inside the rally, not a full blow-off.
  3. 实现波动偏高而月末IV与RV大致匹配；下月重点核对流入是否延续、拥挤租是否升温，以及高波动是否降温。Realized vol was elevated while end-month IV roughly matched RV; next month check whether inflows persist, crowding rent heats up, and whether high vol cools.
