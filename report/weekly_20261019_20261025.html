IV 对 RV · BTC · ETHIV vs RV · BTC · ETH

BTC 实现波动年化约 29.3%（中等），期末 IV 约 38.8%，IV-RV 价差约 +9.5 点，标签期权偏贵。ETH RV ~32.5%、IV ~53.6%，溢价更宽。中等实现波动并未换来便宜期权。BTC realized vol ~29.3% ann. (mid), end IV ~38.8%, IV–RV spread ~+9.5 pts—options rich. ETH RV ~32.5% vs IV ~53.6%, wider premium. Mid realized vol did not deliver cheap options.

读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
IV 是期权市场对未来波动的定价，RV 是已发生路径的波动；溢价表示保险偏贵。高位震荡周若方向迟迟不来，贵 IV 更像在为「可能破位」付费，而非为已实现的中等波动买单。IV prices expected future vol; RV measures the path already taken. A premium means insurance is expensive. In a high-chop week that withholds direction, rich IV looks more like payment for a possible break than for the mid realized moves already printed.

本期组合：This week: 中等 RV + 偏贵 IV + 高位震荡：波动预算不低、方向预算不足。对依赖趋势的策略不友好，对波动收割与均值回归类框架更合拍——前提是溢价不会因突发行情瞬间蒸发。Mid RV + rich IV + high chop: vol budget is not low, directional budget is thin. Unfriendly to pure trend frameworks; better aligned with vol-harvest and mean-reversion logic—if the premium does not vanish in a sudden break.

价格周跌约 2.4% 的同时 IV 仍高于 RV 近 10 个点，说明下跌尚未被定价成「波动已经释放完毕」。偏贵期权与温和负收益并存，更像区间内的再定价摩擦，而非恐慌式波动尖峰。Price fell ~2.4% while IV stayed nearly 10 pts above RV— the decline was not priced as “vol already fully spent.” Rich options beside a mild negative return look like range re-pricing friction rather than a panic vol spike.

VIX 窗口约 -2.7%，股指波动预期在降温；BTC IV 却相对自身 RV 偏贵。传统与加密波动定价松绑，呼应「加密偏独立」标签，而非同一根恐惧交易。VIX eased ~-2.7% while BTC IV remained rich to its own RV. TradFi and crypto vol pricing loosened—consistent with the crypto-idiosyncratic label, not one shared fear trade.
