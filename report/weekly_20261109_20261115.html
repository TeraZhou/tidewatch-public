读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
高 RV + IV 折价，常见于已实现路径已经很吵、而远期报价尚未把同一噪声全额写进权利金；它描述的是相对定价位置，不是下一周方向说明书。High RV with IV at a discount often means the realized path already made noise while forward quotes have not fully prepaid the same noise into premium—it is a relative pricing map, not a next-week direction manual.

本期组合：This week: 组合上看：趋势向下、波动已实现偏高、期权却相对便宜，同时杠杆更像换手而非单边加码——三者叠在一起，更接近「趋势已发生、拥挤未简单用 OI 表达」的周，而不是低波静默市。Combined: downtrend, elevated realized vol, relatively cheap options, and leverage that looks like churn rather than one-sided add—together a week where trend already happened and crowding is not simply written in OI, not a quiet low-vol tape.

价格深跌的同时 IV 并未飙到显著溢价区，反而相对 RV 折价；说明本周波动更多被「走出来」而非被期权市场提前标成恐慌溢价。Deep price drop without IV racing into rich premium—instead a discount to RV—suggests more of the week’s vol was walked out in spot/perp path than prepaid as option panic.
