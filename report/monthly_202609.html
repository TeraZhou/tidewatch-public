二、杠杆怎么读2. Leverage read

资金费率 · OI · 清算 · 永续成交额Funding · OI · Liq · Perp turnover

杠杆侧本月更像「上涨时被挤压、整体仓位在卸」而不是持续拥挤加码。期末资金费率年化大约 +8.8%，借多头的「拥挤租」并不极端。未平仓合约规模全月约 -11.7%，说明杠杆总量在收缩。清算笔数峰值出现在 9 月 21 日附近（约 1973 笔量级），与月内几次向上修复段重合，像是空头或反向仓被挤掉、价格被短暂推高，而不是单纯多头狂欢。永续合约成交额合计约 3435 亿美金、现货约 379 亿美金，热闹程度正常偏活跃，但量能更多服务震荡与出清，而不是单边趋势加杠杆。读法上：拥挤并不重，出清与挤压更明显，量能跟上价格来回。Leverage this month looked more like “squeezed on the way up, overall books lightening” than sustained crowded longs. End-of-month funding annualized around +8.8%—the “rent” for being long was not extreme. Open interest fell about -11.7% over the month, so total leverage stock contracted. Liquidation-count peaks clustered near 21 Sep (order of ~1973), lining up with upside repair legs—more shorts/opposite books getting squeezed than a pure long party. Perp turnover summed about $343.5B and spot about $37.9B: activity was normal-to-busy, but volume served chop and de-leveraging more than one-way trend leverage. Read: crowding was mild, clear-out and squeeze clearer, volume tracked the two-way path.

四、波动环境4. Volatility regime

IV 对 RV · BTC · ETHIV vs RV · BTC · ETH

本月实现波动偏高：比特币年化实现波动大约 41%，期末期权隐含波动约 35%，隐含相对实现折价约 6 个百分点。以太坊实现约 44%、隐含约 49%。价格来回幅度不小，但期权定价并未明显贵过已经发生的波动。Realized vol ran elevated this month: Bitcoin realized around 41% annualized, end implied near 35%, so implied sat about 6 points cheap to realized. Ether realized about 44% with implied near 49%. Moves were sizable, yet options were not priced rich versus the volatility that already printed.

读法 · 后视镜 vs 保险报价How to read · rear-view vs insurance
实现波动大致是「这个月价格实际晃得有多厉害」的年化读数；隐含波动是期权市场「接下来愿意买保险的价格」。两者一高一低，能看出保险贵不贵。Realized vol is a rough annualized gauge of how hard price actually swung this month; implied vol is what the options market charges for insurance ahead. Comparing them shows whether that insurance looks expensive or cheap.

本月组合：This month: 合起来看：实际晃得不小（实现偏高），但期末保险相对便宜（隐含低于实现）。高位震荡里这种组合，常对应「波动已经释放过一轮，期权尚未重新抬价」。Together: the tape actually moved a lot (realized high), yet end-of-month insurance looked relatively cheap (implied below realized). In high-level chop that mix often means vol already printed while options have not fully re-priced up.

价格全月仍收红、路径震荡，隐含波动却落在实现之下。说明上涨与来回并没有把期权抬成「很贵」的状态，更多是波动兑现后的折价。Price finished green for the month on a choppy path while implied sat under realized—gains and two-way action did not leave options looking expensive; more a post-print discount.

传统侧 VIX 本月几乎走平，美股波动温吞；加密实现波动仍明显高于这一水平。两边风险偏好同向，但加密自己的晃动幅度更大。Traditional VIX was essentially flat—equity vol stayed mild—while crypto realized vol remained clearly higher. Risk appetite moved together, but crypto’s own swing size was larger.
